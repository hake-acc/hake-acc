# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary Users**: Established YouTube creators and gaming channel operators (10K to 2M+ reach), primarily within gaming and Minecraft creator ecosystems.
- **User Situation**: Fast-growing channels where community management, Discord moderation, video editing handoffs, and brand deal communications have become an overwhelming operational burden taking time away from creating content.
- **Job to Be Done**: Outsource server architecture, moderation workflows, video editor pipelines, and sponsor negotiations to a reliable, trusted coordinator who speaks native creator language.

## Product Purpose

- **Core Mission**: Provide a grounded, authentic, and high-conversion portfolio for Hake, establishing authority as an indispensable creator services coordinator and Discord architect.
- **Definition of Success**: Creators quickly recognize authentic credibility, explore managed creator hubs, understand the 6 core deliverables, and initiate contact directly via Discord or Twitter.

## Positioning

- **Core Mechanism**: "I manage. You create. We grow together." — An operational backbone run through a proven network of trusted professionals, with zero corporate buzzwords, zero inflated benchmarks, and 100% real creator case studies.
- **Differentiator**: Deep personal immersion in Discord server structures and creator dynamics, offering hands-on execution rather than theoretical agency consulting.

## Operating Context

- **Client Workflows**: Asynchronous communication primarily through Discord direct messages and community servers, rapid review rounds via Twitter/X, and occasional structured email briefs.
- **Technical Stack**: Static-first web architecture (Astro, TypeScript, Tailwind/Vanilla CSS tokens, Playwright testing, Vercel deployment), optimized for instant load times and zero render latency.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Core Deliverables**:
  1. Social Media Management (multi-platform scheduling and community distribution).
  2. Sponsorships & Brand Deals (brand communication, negotiation, and closing).
  3. Discord Server Setup & Architecture (roles, bot automation, channel permissions, community moderation).
  4. Content Editing Coordination (YouTube videos, shorts, and thumbnails via skilled editor network).
  5. Strategy & Content Planning (upload pacing and format iteration).
  6. Reporting & Analytics (performance metrics and community growth insights).
- **Constraints**:
  - No fabricated statistics or inflated vanity claims.
  - Humanized copy; reject corporate buzzwords and generic AI jargon.
  - Authentic asset representation; never crop out or occlude hero design elements (coffee mug, laptop, drawing pencil, flying plane).

## Brand Commitments

- **Name & Identity**: `hake.` (lowercase with electric blue brand period).
- **Tone of Voice**: Clear, direct, humble, reliable, and conversational ("No fluff, no corporate buzzwords — just reliable execution").
- **Visual Identity**:
  - Dual-mode support: Clean daylight mode and Discord-inspired dark theme (`#313338`).
  - Key Scene Creatives: Anime-style creator working at laptop, Discord coffee mug, desk succulent, drawing pencil, paper plane, electric blue highlights.
  - Tactile UI: Custom sky-to-dark-blue uplifted 3D gradient buttons, crisp typography, and responsive bento layouts.

## Evidence on Hand

- **Client Creator Hubs**: Managed servers for prominent creators including CappuMCXD, Alex Adi XD, MC Thunder Playz, Arpan Gamzo, Not Vangid, and Sky Plays.
- **Reach Metrics**: 25+ creator communities managed with 100K+ combined community reach and 24h direct Discord turnaround.
- **Authentic Assets**: Locally hosted, transparent-processed creator avatars, custom vector icons, and authentic illustrated scene assets.

## Product Principles

1. **Authenticity Over Hype**: Every metric and service claim must reflect real, verifiable creator work.
2. **Respect the Creator's Time**: Information architecture must be effortlessly scannable, leading directly to direct Discord/Twitter outreach without friction.
3. **Pristine Craft & Zero Obstruction**: Visual design elements and floating cards must harmonize with the central artwork without covering individual creatives.
4. **Native Responsiveness**: Flawless visual hierarchy and ergonomic touch targets from compact mobile (360px) to ultra-wide displays (1920px+).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Adherence to WCAG AA contrast standards across both light mode and Discord dark mode.
- 44px+ touch target minimums on all interactive elements across mobile viewports.
- Non-reliant on color alone for interactive status (visual labels and tactile states).
