# Product Overview

**Problem Statement**  
Creative professionals (developers, designers, digital artists) need a portfolio that transcends static grids and instead immerses visitors in a narrative‑driven, cinematic environment.

**Solution Summary**  
A Next.js‑based personal portfolio website that presents a living pixel‑art world as a full‑screen animated backdrop, with a lightweight, elegant UI floating above. The experience emphasizes storytelling, dark atmospheric aesthetics, and seamless micro‑interactions while remaining performant and accessible.

**Target Audience**  
- Independent developers, designers, and digital creators seeking to showcase work in an engaging manner.  
- Employers and recruiters looking for polished, memorable candidate presentations.  
- Fans of indie‑game‑style visual storytelling who appreciate retro‑futuristic design.

**Core Value Proposition**  
- Immersive cinematic pixel‑art world that frames all content.  
- Dark, atmospheric palette with high contrast for readability.  
- Story‑first layout that guides users through projects, skills, and contact actions.  
- Full‑screen responsive design with dark‑mode toggle, SEO‑optimized routing, and WCAG‑AA accessibility.

**Measurable Success Goals**  
1. Achieve a Lighthouse Performance score ≥ 90 on mobile.  
2. Maintain an average session duration ≥ 2 minutes (↑ 30 % vs. baseline).  
3. Reduce bounce rate to ≤ 20 % through engaging micro‑interactions.  
4. Generate ≥ 15 % conversion from visitors to contact‑form submissions.  
5. Rank within the top 3 search results for “personal portfolio developer” within 6 months.
